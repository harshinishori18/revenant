The scoreboard

Three approaches, run over the exact same failed payments, under identical conditions. Any difference between the bars is caused by the approach itself and nothing else.

What am I looking at?

Every bar is the same 8,000 failed payments handled a different way. Do nothing is the floor — abandon them all. Fixed retry is what most businesses do today: wait 30 minutes, try again, give up after three tries. Revenant chooses a different wait and method for each payment. The thin white bracket on each bar is the margin of error, so you can see the gap is real and not luck.

Why one fixed waiting time can never work

Each line shows how likely a payment is to succeed depending on how long you wait before trying again. The lines point in opposite directions — which is exactly why a single rule leaves money behind.

Read this in one line

If the customer's internet dropped, they are still on the checkout page — try again in seconds. If their account was empty, no amount of retrying helps until money arrives — wait hours. One fixed timer cannot serve both, and every business using one is losing whichever half it is tuned against.
